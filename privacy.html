Privacy notice

Last updated: 20 October 2026.

Merivvaro Partners is an independent business-introduction service operating in Greater Manchester. For privacy enquiries, contact merivvaropartners@gmail.com.

Information we receive

When you email us, we receive the details you choose to share, which may include your name, business contact details, property location and the service you need.

How we use information

We use enquiries to respond to you, assess potential contractor introductions and manage related business correspondence. Where an introduction is appropriate, we will discuss sharing relevant enquiry details with a proposed contractor.

Our basis for processing

Depending on the enquiry, processing may be necessary to take steps at your request before an arrangement, or for our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries and coordinating requested introductions. We consider the nature of the information and your reasonable expectations.

Sharing and retention

We may share relevant information with a contractor when arranging an introduction, and with email or technical service providers involved in our operations. We retain correspondence only for as long as reasonably needed for enquiries, records and applicable legal obligations. We do not publish enquiry details on this website.

Your rights

You may contact us to request access, correction or deletion of your personal information, or raise an objection or concern. UK residents can also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Website technology

This static website does not include an on-site contact form or our own advertising or analytics tracking scripts. The hosting provider may process technical access information under its own policies.
